# Design Review 1 (PDR): Nyx Ground Segment

Independent review of `docs/design.md` (draft for Gate 2, 2026-11-13), checked against the spec (`Nyx Ground Segment – … Acceptance.md`, "spec"), `docs/requirements.md`, `docs/assumptions.md`, and research notes R2–R11 where the design cites them. Reviewer did not author the design. Every finding quotes the text it is based on.

## 1. Verdict

**PASS WITH CONDITIONS.** The architecture is sound and the Gate 2 thin slice (sim → one worker → store → SSE table) can start now; none of the findings below touch it. Five MAJOR findings must be closed in the design before Gate 3 work on alarms and commanding starts. Two would fail ATP-02/ATP-03 as written (no ack path into the alarm engine; ATP-03 cannot produce the "not verified in 15 s" case). The cut list cuts two must-requirements against its own rule, and the budget does not close.

## 5. What the design does well

- Traceability is real: every decision has why, alternative, requirement and source, and §6 maps all 47 IDs.
- The frame path is correct by construction: the idempotent key `(vehicle, t_onboard, seq)`, XACK after COMMIT, and the advisory lock held on the writing session (lease and fence in one primitive) together cover ING-03/05/07 without a second lock system.
- DAT-06 becomes a list comparison (D-REP-05) because one pure `step()` serves live and replay, with limits replayed by recorded hash.
- Hazardous confirmation is enforced server-side and bound to an args hash (D-CMD-06), and FAILED carries an `outcome` that answers Q3 honestly.
- The measurement plan is unusually rigorous: one clock domain, n ≥ 59 for p95, raw CSV, and pass/fail at the specified rate kept separate from the 100 Hz stress run.

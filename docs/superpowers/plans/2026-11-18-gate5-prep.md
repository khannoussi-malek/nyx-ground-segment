### Task 2: Redis write-cost test that measures Redis, not the runner (D-ING-07)

**Files:** `tests/integration/test_redis_config.py`, `tests/support/redisx.py` (`timed_serial_xadds`), `tests/unit/test_xadd_rule.py` (new), `docs/design.md` D-ING-07 (one clause).

Ruling for this plan (owner may veto at review): the claim is that the steady-state cost of `appendfsync always` fits one 100 Hz tick. A batch of 1 000 serial XADDs hit by a runner-wide disk stall says nothing about Redis. Rule: up to 3 batches of 1 000; pass when one batch has p99 < 10 ms; print every batch's p50/p99; fail when all 3 exceed it.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing unit test** `test_best_batch_rule`: a pure helper `xadd_bound_met(batches: list[list[float]], p99_max: float = 0.010) -> bool` — `[[0.02]*1000, [0.001]*1000]` → True; `[[0.02]*1000]*3` → False; a batch whose 990th sorted value is 0.0101 → that batch fails.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Implement the helper; the integration test runs batches lazily (stops at the first passing batch) and prints all measured ones.
- [ ] **Step 3:** D-ING-07 gets "(best of up to 3 batches of 1 000 on shared CI disks)".
- [ ] **Step 4: Commit** `test: judge the XADD cost on its best of three batches (shared runner disks)`.
